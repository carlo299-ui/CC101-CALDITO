# Mission Reflection

## Mission 3 – Multi-Cloud Explorer

Completing this laboratory helped me understand that AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud are not exactly the same even though they provide many similar cloud services. The platform that impressed me the most was **Amazon Web Services (AWS)** because of its wide range of services. While researching AWS, I noticed that it provides many options for computing, storage, databases, networking, security, and application development. This makes it useful for different types of organizations and projects.

AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud have many similarities. All three platforms provide virtual machines, storage, databases, networking, identity management, monitoring, and other cloud services. They also use a global infrastructure that allows organizations to deploy applications and resources in different locations. The main difference is that each provider has different strengths and approaches to their services.

Business requirements have a major effect on cloud platform selection. For example, a university that already uses Windows Server, Microsoft 365, and Active Directory may benefit more from Azure because of its Microsoft integration. On the other hand, an AI research company may prefer Google Cloud because of its AI, machine learning, and high-performance computing capabilities. A startup or global e-commerce company may choose AWS because of its wide range of services and scalability.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that choosing a cloud provider is not simply about selecting the most popular platform. I need to look at the client's budget, existing technology, performance requirements, scalability, security, location, and future plans before making a recommendation.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio also improved after completing this mission. I added organized documentation about AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud and created comparisons and recommendations based on different business scenarios. This activity also helped me practice using Markdown and organizing technical information in a professional way.

Overall, this mission gave me a better understanding of how cloud engineers and solutions architects make technology decisions based on actual business requirements.
